# CaseQuarry Contributor Rights Terms — DRAFT

**Working discussion draft for legal counsel. Do not treat it as an operative Contributor License Agreement or demand consent to unfinished terms.**

## Purpose

CaseQuarry is source-available, free-to-use software that does **not** generally permit users to make independent modified products. Nevertheless, the copyright owner wants community bug fixes, enhancements, documentation and test contributions in the **official upstream repository**.

If a pull request contains independently copyrighted new code, the original developer retains rights in that work unless an effective written assignment is agreed. For the official maintainer to distribute updates under the chosen restricted license, the maintainer needs adequate contribution rights. A codebase is not automatically owned entirely by the maintainer after merging pull requests.

## Draft proposed grant (counsel to adapt)

By expressly agreeing to the final contributor terms in the process prescribed by Licensor, each contributor (individual or their duly authorized employer) would grant **[COPYRIGHT OWNER]** a **perpetual, worldwide, non-exclusive, irrevocable (except as required by law), royalty-free, transferable and sublicensable copyright license** to use, reproduce, prepare derivative works of, publicly display, distribute, incorporate into the Software, sell or otherwise commercialize, and license or relicense the Contributor's original accepted contribution in connection with the CaseQuarry project and its successor versions, including under proprietary/source-available license terms. The contributor retains ownership absent a separate executed assignment.

**Review questions for counsel:** patent licensing (if relevant), warranties/representations, employer approval, obligations with contributed code and third-party snippets, contribution acceptance process, scope of sublicense, enforceability, withdrawal, moral rights, governing law and applicable privacy law.

## Contributor declaration (proposed for the approved process)

- I own or control rights required to submit my contribution and make the agreed license grant.
- I have identified included third-party code/dependencies and their licenses.
- No confidential client records, production evidence, trade secrets, credentials or illegally obtained data are included.
- I understand a pull request may be rejected or altered by maintainers.
- I understand that the project's official maintainer may release the accepted contribution under the official source-available terms, subject to the final contributor agreement I accept.

## Maintainer guidance

Create appropriate contributor-agreement workflow artifacts but **do not automatically assert that opening a PR constitutes valid copyright transfer, acceptance of a nonfinal draft, or execution of a final CLA**. Until the owner obtains legal review, label these as proposed and make external contribution acceptance contingent on approved terms.
