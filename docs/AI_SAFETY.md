# Optional analysis policy

AI is disabled by default and is not needed for preservation, keyword search, review or export. Local models are optional separately installed components with their own licences, hardware requirements and operational risks. Remote evidence transmission must be explicitly enabled and case-authorized; this initial release does not imply a validated remote-provider workflow.

Retrieved evidence is untrusted data. A message asking to ignore instructions, send secrets, change a review decision or open another case cannot authorize any action. The model receives bounded authorized source spans and has no shell, network, database or filesystem action tools.

Every material generated finding requires an authorized evidence identifier, immutable text artifact/version and validated character/page/time location. Invalid or outside-scope citations must be rejected. Empty retrieval yields an insufficiency message. Generated findings remain separate from human decisions and cannot silently confirm privilege or facts.

Coverage distinguishes total matches from processed sources, OCR/parse failures and sampling. A source citation demonstrates the passage used, not the truth of the passage or completeness of a case. Professional review remains required.
