# Reviewer guide

Log in and open an authorized matter. Create or select a source/custodian before importing synthetic evaluation files. Watch import states and coverage: preserved, searchable, partial and failed are different counts. Missing OCR or unsupported adapters never establish that evidence is absent.

Search by words/phrases and supported Boolean/metadata expressions; use backend filters, sort, groups and pagination. Group totals refer to the authorized matching set, while a page contains only a bounded subset. Save a reproducible search or review set through the implemented product controls.

Open an item to inspect the extracted content, participants, raw/normalized dates, source occurrence, attachment family and SHA-256. Extraction is a derivative and can omit text; inspect the preserved original when authorized. Remote email assets and scripts must not execute in the viewer.

Record responsiveness, tags, notes and bookmarks as human decisions. Suspected privilege is a review flag and requires professional determination. Review version conflicts mean another reviewer changed the item; reload before overwriting their work.

Verify source hashes and review restricted items before export. Confirm the intended scope and purpose, download only authorized material and validate its manifest. Optional source-cited analysis remains a suggestion with explicit scope and gaps; unsupported or unconfigured models must not fabricate answers.

CaseQuarry assists review. It does not provide legal advice, decide privilege/admissibility or prove that an incomplete collection contains every relevant communication.
